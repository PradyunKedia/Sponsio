# Ethical 5K-View Launch Plan

The target is collective public views across all Blitz posts. Do not buy views, use engagement bots, or make unverifiable claims.

## Content sequence

### Launch hour

1. Publish the 30+ second native demo video on X.
2. Publish the longer LinkedIn launch post with the same native video.
3. Reply to each with the live app, repository, and Monadscan links.
4. Publish the separate vertical creative advertisement.
5. Ask teammates to repost with a personal takeaway instead of identical copy.

### First six hours

- Share in official event channels where project promotion is allowed.
- Ask participating players to quote-post their result or favorite moment.
- Reply to every substantive comment with a short product detail.
- Post one clip focused on the final-ten-second leaderboard movement.
- Tag required accounts only where contextually relevant; do not repeatedly spam them.

### Next day

- Post a short build thread explaining headcount versus effective equity.
- Share an organizer use case: hackathon judging, grant selection, or demo day.
- Publish a result card with unique wallets, completed rooms, and one testimonial.

## Conversion hooks

- Lead with “100 seconds to move a room,” not architecture.
- Put moving gameplay in the first two seconds.
- Burn readable captions into every video.
- End with one action: join a live room or try the public app.
- Keep the room code visible long enough to copy.

## Evidence

Immediately before judging, record:

- URL of every qualifying post.
- Public view count for each post.
- Timestamp and screenshot.
- Collective total.

Copy the links and total into `docs/JUDGING_CHECKLIST.md`. Views are platform-reported and cannot be guaranteed by repository work alone.
