# Sponsio: Pre-Market Fit and Revenue

## Problem

Groups routinely make high-stakes choices with tools that flatten conviction into a single click. Polls do not distinguish early, stable support from last-second bandwagoning. Governance systems often let capital directly buy voting power, while event games rarely produce a reusable decision artifact.

## Initial users

1. Hackathon and conference organizers who need an interactive room-wide event.
2. Ecosystem teams allocating small grants or prizes.
3. DAOs triaging proposals, bounties, or emergency priorities.
4. Developer communities running demo days and project showcases.

## Why they would adopt

- A room can join through a single shared code.
- One-wallet-one-headcount is easy to explain and resists capital-weighted outcomes.
- The live board creates spectacle and audience participation.
- Loyalty and timing reveal conviction that a normal poll discards.
- Monad provides public escrow and a verifiable settlement artifact.

## Beachhead product

Start as a hosted event product:

- Organizer creates a branded Sponsio room.
- Participants submit projects or options.
- The event runs several short rounds.
- Winners receive testnet or sponsored prizes.
- Organizer receives a public result page and action log.

The hackathon version proves the highest-risk behavior: whether a real room enjoys coordinating and switching under a visible countdown.

## Revenue strategy

### Hosted event fee

Charge organizers per event or per active room. Include setup, custom branding, projector mode, and result exports.

### Protocol fee

For paid public rooms, route a configurable percentage of the pool to the protocol treasury. This requires a reviewed mainnet contract revision and explicit fee disclosure.

### Subscription

Offer communities recurring rooms, analytics, moderation, saved templates, and custom domains.

### White-label licensing

Provide embeddable coordination tournaments to wallets, accelerators, and ecosystem teams.

## Illustrative pricing

- Community room: free or sponsored.
- Hosted event: $250–$1,000 depending on attendance and support.
- Community subscription: $99–$499 per month.
- White-label deployment: setup fee plus usage.

These are hypotheses, not established revenue. Validate them through organizer interviews and paid pilots.

## Evidence to collect today

- Unique wallets that successfully joined.
- Number and size of completed rooms.
- Percentage of players who switched at least once.
- Repeat-play rate.
- Organizer testimonial.
- Participant quote describing why the live room was more engaging than a poll.
- Requests to reuse Sponsio for another event.

## Innovation claim

Do not claim that decision markets or conviction voting are new. Sponsio’s defensible combination is:

- Endogenous outcome: the market state produces the decision.
- Decoupled powers: headcount determines the outcome; effective equity determines payout.
- Anti-bandwagon measurement: loyalty and time decay quantify how stable the winning coalition was.
- Room-scale interaction: the mechanism is optimized for a short, observable, real-time social contest.

## Mainnet path

Mainnet points should only be pursued after:

1. Independent contract review.
2. Explicit protocol-fee and unclaimed-fund policy.
3. Operator key moved to multisig or managed signing infrastructure.
4. Settlement monitoring and challenge policy.
5. Production database and multi-instance room routing.
