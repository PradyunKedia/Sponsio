# 60-Second Sponsio Demo Video

Record at 1080p or higher. Keep the browser zoom large enough for room codes, timers, and Monadscan hashes to remain readable on mobile social feeds.

## Pre-recording

- Deploy and verify the contract.
- Open two MetaMask profiles funded with testnet MON.
- Open the app in two browser windows.
- Prepare the public repository and Monadscan contract tabs.
- Turn off notifications and hide private keys, seed phrases, balances, and unrelated tabs.
- Complete one rehearsal and confirm microphone levels.

## Shot list and narration

### 0–5 seconds

Visual: Sponsio landing page and Create Room.

Narration: “Sponsio turns a room’s conviction into a decision—in one hundred seconds.”

### 5–15 seconds

Visual: Create a room, copy the code, and join from a second MetaMask wallet.

Narration: “Two to one hundred real players enter with MetaMask on Monad Testnet and pitch what they built.”

### 15–28 seconds

Visual: Start the room, show both profiles, and switch support.

Narration: “Every wallet has equal headcount, so money cannot buy the result. You can switch at any time, but indecision burns effective equity.”

### 28–42 seconds

Visual: Show the synchronized leaderboard and timer in both windows.

Narration: “WebSockets keep the room instant and synchronized while every action is persisted for settlement.”

### 42–53 seconds

Visual: Show final winner and click Claim Testnet MON.

Narration: “At zero, headcount chooses the winner and loyalty-weighted equity splits the escrowed pool.”

### 53–60 seconds

Visual: Open the claim on Monadscan, then show the GitHub README.

Narration: “The escrow, settlement root, and claim are public on Monad. This is Sponsio.”

## Export

- Duration: 45–75 seconds.
- Format: MP4, H.264, 1080p.
- Include captions.
- First frame must contain the Sponsio name.
- Last frame must contain `[LIVE_URL]`.
- Publish as a native upload rather than only linking externally.
