Today we built Sponsio, a real-time coordination market on Monad.

Most polls tell you what won. Sponsio also measures how strongly the room believed in it. Every participant has equal influence over the outcome through headcount, while loyalty and timing determine their share of the reward pool.

During a 100-second round, 2–100 people connect MetaMask, submit their projects, and coordinate live. The backend synchronizes the room over WebSockets, while Monad Testnet escrows stakes and settles claims through a public Merkle root.

## Follow-up thread

1. Why headcount? Capital cannot buy the outcome. One wallet remains one vote.
2. Why loyalty decay? Switching stays possible, but bandwagoning dilutes the switcher's payout.
3. Why Monad? The live room needs fast confirmation and inexpensive, EVM-compatible settlement.
4. Why hybrid? WebSockets give 100 phones an immediate synchronized experience; Monad provides public escrow and claims.
5. What comes next? Hosted tournaments, grant curation, and white-label coordination rooms.
