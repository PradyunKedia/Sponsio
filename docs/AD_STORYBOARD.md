# Creative Advertisement: “The Last Ten Seconds”

Format: vertical 9:16, 25–35 seconds, fast arcade sound design.

## Concept

A boring poll asks people to choose and wait. Sponsio turns the same decision into a live social contest where alliances move until the final second.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The dead poll

Visual: Grey screen reading “Option A: 51%, Option B: 49%.”

Voiceover: “Polls show the answer. They hide the conviction.”

Sound: One dull click.

### Scene 2 — Insert coin

Visual: Hard cut to the Sponsio arcade landing page. Create Room flashes.

Voiceover: “Insert Sponsio.”

Sound: Arcade coin and startup tone.

### Scene 3 — The room moves

Visual: Rapid cuts of MetaMask connecting, callsigns appearing, the room code spreading, and the live leaderboard changing.

On-screen text: “ONE WALLET. ONE HEADCOUNT.”

Voiceover: “Rally the room. Back a build. Change your mind—but every switch costs conviction.”

### Scene 4 — Final countdown

Visual: Timer moves from 00:10 to 00:00 while two profiles trade first place.

On-screen text: “LOYALTY PAYS.”

Sound: Accelerating eight-bit heartbeat.

### Scene 5 — Proof

Visual: Winner screen, Claim Testnet MON, then Monadscan transaction.

Voiceover: “The room decides. Monad settles.”
