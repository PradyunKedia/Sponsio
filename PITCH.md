# Sponsio Demo and Judging Runbook

## 45-second pitch

Sponsio turns a room full of opinions into a decision with measurable conviction. Two to one hundred people connect MetaMask on Monad Testnet, enter a project, and back one another during a 100-second live round. Headcount decides which project wins, so capital cannot buy the vote. Switching remains possible, but loyalty and time decay reduce the switcher's payout share. The backend gives every phone a synchronized real-time experience, while Monad escrows the testnet MON pool and settles winner claims with a public Merkle root.

It is original because voting power and payout power are deliberately separated: every wallet remains one vote, while early conviction earns more of the prize. It has pre-market-fit for hackathon judging, grant selection, bounty triage, and rapid DAO decisions. Revenue can come from hosted event fees, configurable protocol fees on paid rooms, and white-label coordination tournaments.

## Live demo sequence

1. Open the public GitHub repository and README.
2. Open the public Sponsio page and show `MONAD TESTNET`.
3. Create a room and copy its invite link.
4. Join from a second MetaMask wallet.
5. Show both profiles arrive live without refreshing.
6. Start the room with its actual player count.
7. Switch support and show both screens plus the projector update.
8. At freeze, show the winner and effective-equity calculation.
9. Claim testnet MON from the winning wallet.
10. Open the claim transaction and verified Sponsio source on Monadscan.
